# Skills by role type

The skills Skill Proof looks for, ranked by how often real job postings ask for them. Generated from 3,960 English-language postings collected in November 2026 from the public Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Remotive, Arbeitnow and Remote OK feeds. LinkedIn, Indeed and Naukri were not scraped: they block it and their terms forbid it.

O*NET 30.2 (US Department of Labor, CC BY 4.0) was used as an independent check for missing tools.

**How to read this:** the percentage is the share of postings of that role type that name the skill, after removing company, benefits and legal sections. Role types with few postings are marked; treat those rankings as a rough guide. These feeds lean towards tech and remote employers, so healthcare, education and trades are under-represented.
