Your data

Your resume stays yours.

Skill Proof checks your resume on your own device. We don't ask you to sign up, we don't keep your resume, and we don't train AI on it. Here is exactly what happens, including the one feature that does use AI.

No account Nothing saved on our servers No tracking or analytics Matching runs on your device
Matching

Your skill check happens in your browser

Finding skills, sorting them into Backed, Only listed, Different phrase and Missing, the role insights, resume checks and the ATS view are all worked out by code running on your device. Your resume and the job description are never sent anywhere to do this.

When you upload a PDF or Word file, it is read on your device too. The page downloads two free file readers when it opens, but your file is never uploaded.

Storage

Kept only in this browser tab

So a refresh doesn't wipe your work, the page keeps your text in this tab's own storage. It never reaches us, and your browser deletes it when you close the tab. Choose Start fresh to clear it straight away.

AI bullets

"Suggest a bullet" is the only part that uses AI

AI never decides whether you have a skill. It only drafts a bullet, and only when you click Suggest a bullet on a skill. For that one request, we send the least it needs to write something true to your experience:

Sent

  • The skill you clicked
  • The job description's wording for it
  • The role
  • Your experience bullet points

Never sent

  • Your name and header
  • Email, phone number and links
  • Summary, education and skills list
  • Job-title lines with dates
  • The job description itself

Before anything goes out, we remove any email address, phone number or link that appears inside a bullet, on your device and again on our server. Words you wrote inside a bullet, such as a company or client name, are sent as written. Our server passes the request to the AI model and keeps nothing.

AI training

Does anyone train AI on my resume?

Skill Proof doesn't. We don't build or train AI models, and we don't keep your text to do so later. Your skill check never touches AI at all.

The bullet writer runs on NVIDIA's model. It never receives your name or contact details, and NVIDIA doesn't store your request once your bullet is written. NVIDIA's terms do allow it to use anonymised requests to improve its models.

If you'd rather nothing at all leaves your device, skip "Suggest a bullet". Everything else works exactly the same without it.

From the NVIDIA API Trial Terms of Service: §2.3 "NVIDIA will not store or use User Content or Generated Content at the end of each API Service session" and §3.3 "User Content and Generated Content to improve NVIDIA products and services, including AI models", collected "without identifying specific users". Stay safe

Using Skill Proof safely

  • Use the skill check freely. It never sends your resume anywhere.
  • Before you click "Suggest a bullet", take out anything confidential from your bullets, like client names or unreleased projects.
  • Read every suggested bullet and only keep what's true. The AI can't know your work better than you do.
  • Close the tab, or choose Start fresh, when you're done on a shared computer.
Anything else

What every website sees

Like any site, our host (Vercel) and Google Fonts, which supplies the typeface, see your IP address and browser type when the page loads. They never see what you type or upload.
